  • Receipts for reimbursement

FAQ

Team questions

Can subcontractors use the app?
Yes. An outside company is an assignee like any other, and its people can see the work shared with them.
Is it the same app for everyone?
Yes. There is one FirstMate app; what each person sees depends on their roles and what your organization turns on.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes, on both. Download the app.
