How it works

One form builder for every kind of request.

Steps made of blocks.

A form is a few steps, and each step holds blocks: contact, address, choices, dates, a property measurement or an appointment. Show a block only when an earlier answer calls for it. Start from a template or ask the assistant to build one.

  • Contact, address, select, number, date and consent blocks
  • Show a block or step based on earlier answers
  • Your logo, colors and confirmation email

Instant quotes from a real measurement.

Measure the property from the address the visitor types, then price it with your own table: a quantity, priced options and adjustments. If something cannot be priced, the lead still comes in and the visitor is told pricing will follow.

  • Your price table, your options
  • Quantities from answers or measurements
  • The estimate is saved on the lead

Bookings go straight on the schedule.

An appointment block uses one of your appointment types, so duration, arrival window, staffing and working hours are the ones your office already uses. Visitors only see times you can actually keep.

  • Open times planned against the visitor's address
  • Minimum notice and how far ahead to book
  • Assignees, confirmation and reminders like any appointment

FAQ

Questions

Can I use forms on my existing website?
Yes. Each published form has an embed you can paste into any site, or add it to a page in the FirstMate Website Builder.
What if the time a customer picked gets taken?
The time is checked again on submit. If it is gone, the visitor picks another before anything is created.
Do I have to show prices?
No. Pricing is optional. A form can just collect details and book a visit.
