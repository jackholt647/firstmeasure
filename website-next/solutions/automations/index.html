FAQ

Questions

Do I have to build automations from scratch?
No. FirstMate comes with industry defaults you can change, and the assistant can write or adjust automations from a plain description.
What happens to jobs already running when I change an automation?
They keep the version they started with until you preview and push the change to them.
Will a customer get the same text twice?
Each action runs once per event, even if the event is delivered more than once.
Can a follow-up stop when the customer replies?
Yes. A sequence ends when its step is completed, and a reply can complete it.
