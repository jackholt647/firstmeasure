FAQ

Checklist questions

Can a checklist require photos?
Yes. Any item can require photos or video, a document, or an audio note before it can be completed.
Do checklists appear on projects automatically?
Yes. Scope filters decide which checklists a project gets, based on the kind of work it includes.
Can homeowners see checklists?
Only the ones you choose to share with them.
