Nothing is marked paid without a real charge.

  • Bank payments stay pending until they settle
  • Declines are never recorded
  • Refund to the original card or bank account

FAQ

Questions about payments

Who processes the payments?
FirstMate takes card and bank payments through an integrated payment processor. You apply once and, when approved, can take payments from every job. Placeholder: rates and approval details.
Can I still take cash and checks?
Yes. Record them from the job as cash or check with a reference, and they count toward the balance like any other payment.
When does an ACH payment count as paid?
A bank payment shows as pending until it settles. If it is returned, the payment is reversed on the job automatically.
Do my people need to handle card numbers?
No. Card and bank details are typed into the processor's secure field and never touch your FirstMate account.
