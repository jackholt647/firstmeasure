FAQ

Questions about Full House

Which structures are included?
Any building on the property you confirm when you order: the house, detached garages, sheds, barns, pool houses and so on. You can skip any of them.
How is it different from Full Exterior?
Full Exterior measures the house. Full House measures every structure on the property in the same order, with numbers for each.
