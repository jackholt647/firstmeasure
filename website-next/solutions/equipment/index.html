Questions

What counts as equipment?
Anything you schedule or maintain: vehicles, trailers, lifts, generators, measuring tools. You define the types.
Does equipment show on the schedule?
Yes. Equipment is a resource on schedule events, and conflicts are flagged before you save.
Can I track rented equipment?
Yes. Each unit can carry purchase, lease or rental details.
