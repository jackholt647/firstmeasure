FAQ

Questions

Can the assistant see things I'm not allowed to see?
No. Every tool call re-checks the signed-in person's current permissions, the same way the apps do.
Will it message customers on its own?
It drafts, and asks before it sends or changes work. Company instructions can't override those confirmation rules.
Where does it remember things?
In a short list of memories you own. You can add, edit or clear them, or turn memory off, in settings.
