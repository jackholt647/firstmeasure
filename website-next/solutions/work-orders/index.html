Completion

Confirm the work, pay it out, lock it.

When the job is done, confirm the lines and finalize the list. Finalized work is locked and becomes "to be paid" for piece-rate pay. Pay out in parts if the job is finished in stages; only a manager can reopen it, always with a reason.

  • One approval, select all, or line by line
  • Pay out part of a work order now, the rest later
  • Reopen with a recorded reason

Closeout

Close the job with a checklist, not a hunch.

Closeout walks through what is still open across the project: change orders, signatures, payments, receipts and requirements. It warns rather than blocks; the only thing that stops a close is a work order not yet finalized.

  • Checks read from every app on the project
  • Finalize open work orders right in the dialog
  • Final expenses, planned against actual

FAQ

Work order questions

Can a work order be paid out in parts?
Yes. Confirm some lines and pay those now; the rest stay open until they are done or cancelled.
Can finished work be changed later?
A finalized list is locked. Someone with permission can reopen it, and the reason is recorded.
Does closeout stop me from closing a job?
Closeout warns about anything open, but only an unfinalized work order stops the close.
