Questions

Does my partner need FirstMate?
Connected partners each work in their own FirstMate organization. Placeholder: confirm how partners without an account join.
Can a partner see my whole project?
No. They see only the items you share with them, with the access you choose, and you can revoke it any time.
Can I schedule a subcontractor that isn't connected?
Yes. Add them as an outside company and assign work to them like any other assignee.
