FAQ

Questions about payroll

Does FirstMate run payroll taxes?
No. FirstMate works out who earned what and when, and hands each pay run to the payroll or accounting software you already use.
What if a job changes after it was paid?
A refresh posts only the difference as a new line. Posted earnings and paid runs are never edited.
Can different subcontractors have different rates?
Yes. Every assignee has their own pay sheet, so two companies on the same roof can be paid entirely differently.
