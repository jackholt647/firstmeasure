FAQ

Scheduling questions

Can I schedule outside companies as well as my own people?
Yes. Users, groups of users and outside companies are all assignees, and any of them can be put on an event alongside equipment.
What happens when I book someone twice?
By default FirstMate warns you and shows the overlap. Your organization, or a single assignee, can be set to block or to allow overlaps instead.
Do my people see changes on their phones?
Yes. The schedule in the FirstMate app updates as soon as an event is saved.
Can one job have several stages on the calendar?
Yes. Stages are child events of the job; they keep their own people and equipment and move with the job.
