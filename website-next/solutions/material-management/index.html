FAQ

Material questions

Where do the quantities come from?
From the project's measurements and the rules you set for each product, including waste. You can also add or edit lines by hand.
Can I send orders to my supplier?
Yes. A material list becomes a material order with delivery instructions; several lists can be combined into one order.
What if the measurements change?
Regenerate the list and the quantities follow. Lines your team added or changed by hand are told apart from generated ones.
