FAQ

Questions about invoicing

Can I invoice part of a job?
Yes. Invoice any milestone or custom amount; the project shows what has been billed to date and what is left.
Can customers pay the invoice online?
When your account can take payments, the invoice email and customer portal include a pay link for card or bank.
What happens to a paid or voided invoice?
It moves to History with its payments, PDF and, for a void, the reason.
