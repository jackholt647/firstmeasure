FAQ

Questions about photos

Who can see a project's photos?
People with access to the project. Homeowners only see the photos you share to their portal.
Do my people need a separate camera app?
No. Photos are taken from the FirstMate app on iPhone or Android and saved to the project.
Can I choose who sees a feed post?
Yes. Each post has an audience, from everyone to a single department.
